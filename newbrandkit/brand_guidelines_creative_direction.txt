# Brand Guidelines & Creative Direction: Caliper



## Brand Identity & Tagline

- Official Brand Name: Caliper

- Official Tagline: "The Sewing & Calibration Atelier"



## Brand Philosophy & Aesthetic Movement

Caliper is a whimsical, nostalgic maker’s atelier and tactile scrapbook workshop. It marries the playful, tactile charm of textile craft (stitched patchwork, running stitches, button hardware, ribbon spools, paperclips) with the loving precision of a tailor's studio. It feels physical, hand-assembled, and storybook-inspired — like stepping into a vintage haberdashery or French confectionery workshop.



## Core Visual Metaphor: The Maker's Workbench

Every visual layout should look like an open, tactile workspace rather than a flat digital screen:

- Manila and pastel workshop file folders with rounded tabs replace rigid digital boxes.

- A spiral-bound or tear-away lined paper pad serves as the conversational area, accented with washi tape, memo slips, and pinned fabric swatches.

- Whimsical, hand-drawn dials with circular rotation arrows and stitched button centers replace sterile mechanical knobs.

- Soft powder blue and white vertical awning stripes frame banners and header borders, providing an artisanal boutique feel.

- Pure crisp white (#FFFFFF) provides a clean, breathable foundation that keeps the whimsical collage elements feeling light, airy, and modern.



## Palette & Color Story

- Base Canvas: Pure Studio White (#FFFFFF) for clean, open, high-breathability backdrops.

- Primary Soft Accent: Powder Blue (#BFD7EA) for awning stripes, soft ribbon bands, and gentle container tints.

- Boutique Warmth: Confectioner's Blush Pink (#FAD2E1) for active folder tabs, playful stickers, and memo slips.

- Natural Fiber Tones: Sage Olive (#79785B) and Sewing Teal (#397772) for organic foliage tags, thimbles, and safety check marks.

- Deep Ink Grounding: Atelier Plum / Espresso (#3D1E28) for hand-drawn ink sketches, strong typography, and borders.

- Signature Sparks: Cherry Red (#BF1318) and Buttercup Gold (#DEA458) reserved for stitched thread needles, pearl-head pins, and button centers.

## Texture & Pattern Balance: The Gingham Rule

- The gingham check pattern is sacred and exclusive: it belongs strictly to the fabric letters of the official Caliper logo.

- Never cover whole cards, headers, or backgrounds in repeating gingham patterns.

- Complement the logo with clean, subtle textures: vertical awning stripes, fine dashed running stitches, faint blueprint grid lines, and smooth cardstock.



## Official Canva Brand Kit Typography Hierarchy

Use the official Caliper typography system exactly as mapped in the brand kit:

- Title: Oslo Stitch (tactile stitched-outline typography for celebratory headers, hero badges, and callouts like "SEW IT!").

- Subtitle: Autography (flowing, expressive cursive script for the official tagline "The Sewing & Calibration Atelier", maker endorsements, and signature flourishes).

- Heading: Photography (distinctive, expressive display font for primary feature titles and collection banners).

- Subheading: Christmas (charming, decorative display font for secondary labels, section intros, and workshop indices).

- Section Header: Pastel Crayon (textured, handcrafted crayon sans-serif for clear structure and section navigation).

- Body: Jupiteroid (clean, friendly, approachable casual hand-drawn font for narrative paragraphs and comfortable reading).

- Quote: Jupiteroid (consistent casual handwritten styling for sticky notes, pattern instructions, and marginal comments).

- Caption: Jupiteroid (legible, compact casual font for machine models, gauge sizes, and technical calibration parameters).



## Illustration & Collage Art Direction

- Line Art Style: Loose, expressive monoline ink drawings (vintage sewing machines, scissors, thread spools, needles).

- Physical Hardware: Realistic or illustrated 4-hole resin buttons, pearl-headed straight pins, paperclips, and black wire binder clips holding paper layers together.

- Tactile Depth: Layer elements with slight organic rotational tilts (1° to 2°) and soft, diffused drop shadows to mimic pieces of paper resting on a table.



## Creative Do's and Don'ts

- DO celebrate the charm of handmade imperfections, stitches, and physical stationery.

- DO keep plenty of white space so playful elements have room to breathe.

- DO use the exact provided Caliper logo asset without modifying or retyping its letters.

- DON'T use cold corporate tech blues, neon cyber gradients, or dark-mode hacker palettes.

- DON'T overload layouts with busy plaid/gingham wallpapers.

- DON'T use stiff, generic stock photography or sterile enterprise icons.
